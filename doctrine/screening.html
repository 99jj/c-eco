Is This a c-ECO Case?

The c-ECO Systemic Governance Regime is meant to apply where institutions need to recognize systemic risk early enough to preserve reversibility and accountable choice — before harm becomes irreversible. At a general level, applicability turns on three conditions converging:

Systemic Risk

The operation affects, or is affected by, systems beyond direct control — ecosystems, supply chains, critical infrastructure, communities.

Invisible Lock-In

There is dependence on assets, technologies, territories or relationships that are difficult to substitute, migrate or reverse.

Continuous Monitorability

It is technically and financially viable to monitor the relevant conditions on an ongoing basis, rather than at a single point in time.

Where all three conditions are present, the case is a candidate for c-ECO governance; where one is clearly absent, traditional risk management or standard contracting is likely sufficient. The applicable pathway, if any, is then a matter for the c-ECO technical and legal instruments.
