TFP Manual — Public Overview

The Threshold Function Protocol Operational Manual translates the normative architecture of the Model Law into concrete, auditable, and operational procedures. It specifies how Γ = f(P, ΔV, σ, Lr) transforms planetary signals into automatic legal effects — through standardized processes for data collection, certification, monitoring, and trigger activation under Foundation stewardship.

ΔV VELOCITY Rate of change

Lr LIQUIDITY Reversibility

Scope of this page
This page is the Public Overview of the TFP Operational Manual — purpose, principles, institutional roles, safeguards, and general governance structure. A separate Controlled Technical Manual (weights, scoring logic, calibration parameters, numeric thresholds, validation procedures, data architecture, and security/deployment detail) governs implementation and is restricted to authorized Foundation, Calibration Council, and certified operator personnel. It is not published on this site.

I

Manual Identity & Legal Nature

This Operational Manual constitutes an integral component of the c-ECO Systemic Governance framework, incorporated by reference into Model Law Art. 215-232. It is not purely regulatory nor merely informational — an intermediate prudential instrument that makes legal automation technically feasible.

Prudential Function

Operationalizes pre-threshold governance duties through standardized procedures that reduce discretion, mitigate strategic behavior, and preserve epistemic integrity.

Binding Through ML

Effects become legally binding only via Model Law incorporation and Foundation certification. Technical output alone creates no legal obligation.

Institutional Legitimacy

Provides due process layer necessary for financial market credibility — the connective tissue between scientific signals, legal effects, and accountability.

II

Risk Mapping to TFP Variables

The Manual provides a framework for translating business risks identified under Screening into the operational TFP variables (P, ΔV, σ, Lr) and their corresponding Model Law legal effects. Recognized risk categories include territorial/spatial risk, technological/infrastructure risk, supply-chain/concentration risk, temporal/cumulative risk, irreversibility risk, community/social-license risk, and financial/fintech hidden digital risk — the last of these treated as a critical domain given the near-instantaneous velocity at which digital and algorithmic harms can propagate.

III

TFP Core Variables — Overview

The Trigger Function is structured around four fundamental variables. Standardized procedures for measuring, certifying, and combining them ensure consistency across sectors and jurisdictions, with Foundation certification as a validity condition.

P
POSITION Safe Operating Space Proximity

Measures distance to relevant biophysical or systemic limits. Not a generic environmental indicator — a boundary-referenced, context-locked metric whose meaning is defined by sectoral specification and calibration.

ΔV
VELOCITY Rate & Direction of Change

Captures the temporal dimension — the direction and rate at which a system moves toward or away from its limits. Primary anticipatory signal: a system far from threshold but accelerating rapidly may present higher danger than one closer but stabilizing.

σ
UNCERTAINTY Asymmetric Prudential Margin

Modulates all other variables, ensuring prudential conservatism. Applied asymmetrically: uncertainty always contracts the operational margin — it is never treated as license for delay or optimism.

Lr
REVERSIBILITY LIQUIDITY Resources Mobilizable Immediately

Assesses the availability and mobilizability of resources required to reverse or contain a harmful trajectory — conceptually, the ratio of resources mobilizable immediately to the projected technical cost of containment.

Trigger Function: Γ = f(P, ΔV, σ, Lr) → prudential score → Band classification → Automatic legal effects

IV

Operational Scores & Band Classification — Overview

From the TFP variables, three operational scores enable classification, comparison, and trigger activation: the Safe Operating Space Proximity Score (SPS), tracking current distance to the applicable boundary; the Trajectory Risk Score (TRS), tracking direction, speed, and persistence of movement toward material limits; and the Reversibility Liquidity Score (RLS), tracking immediate financial capacity to contain, mitigate, restore, or decommission. An operation may be financially solvent yet ecologically insolvent.

GREEN Watch

Enhanced monitoring, no execution restrictions

AMBER Capital Protection

Partial IEX, cash redirection, dividend retention

RED Safe Mode

Execution reconfiguration, not default

BLACK Restoration First

External intervention, automatic asset conversion

Master Rule: No trigger creates new obligations. Activation only gives effect to obligations already contractually pre-agreed. All effects are ex ante, non-discretionary, and scientifically justified.

VII

Restoration Providers & Level 4 Execution — Overview

Restoration Providers are pre-certified technical entities responsible for Black Band / Restoration First execution. Foundation certification ensures their capacity, solvency, and institutional separation from the operators they may act against.

Fiduciary Duty: Restoration Providers hold a fiduciary obligation to systemic preservation, not to the original operators or their creditors. They operate under Technical Curatorship Chamber oversight during Black Band activation.

VIII

Auditability, Transparency & Execution During Review

Continuous QA/QC & Technical Audits

All data flows, calculations, classifications, and trigger activations are subject to continuous Quality Assurance/Quality Control. Oversight is automatically intensified upon procedural deviation detection, statistically significant anomalies, or activation of Amber or higher. Audits are strictly technical — they verify protocol application, not policy merit.

Immutable Public Ledger

All prudential indicators and classifications are recorded in a secure, immutable ledger preserving full data lineage: certified raw inputs, applied methodologies, uncertainty margins, resulting scores, triggered effects, timestamps, and responsible custodians. Public access is proportionate to systemic relevance. Systemic Proof prevails over declaratory evidence.

Execution During Review

The framework adopts the solve et repete principle — where prudential triggers are activated, effects remain operative throughout any audit, review, or arbitral process. Due process is preserved without granting suspensive effect. Arbitral review is limited to procedural failure, evidentiary integrity, fraud, and material measurement error. There is no review of ecological merit, economic desirability, or policy appropriateness.
