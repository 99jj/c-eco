Public doctrine overview

Governance before collapse

The c-ECO doctrine asks how law and institutions can preserve meaningful decision space before ecological or systemic harm becomes irreversible.

Its public foundations include reversibility, precaution, accountable evidence, institutional responsibility and dialogue with Earth System Law.

Internal layers, functions, team assignments, manuals, protocols, scores, triggers, commercial offerings and deployment architecture are not included.
