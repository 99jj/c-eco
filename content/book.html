Public overview

International Edition Index

The c-ECO Systemic Governance Statute is a full international-edition legal instrument spanning 37 parts and 246 chapters, from its founding principles through its operational and technical annexes.

The complete part-by-part and chapter-by-chapter index, including the Threshold Function Protocol's trigger architecture, prudential-band structure, governance and automation chapters, and the associated technical annexes, is not published on this site. The Preamble and Explanatory Memorandum, and the Statute's general foundational chapters, are published separately as part of the public doctrine.
