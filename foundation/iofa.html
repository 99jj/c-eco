Notice of Withdrawal
The Institutional Operational Framework Agreement ("IOFA") is a draft transactional and governance instrument between the Foundation and its designated commercial execution arm. It sets out the proprietary architecture of the c-ECO Framework, the parties' licensing and fee structure, tax and regulatory compliance mechanics across jurisdictions, and related contractual schedules and forms.
Because this material consists of unpublished technical architecture, calibration and licensing terms, financial and fee structures, and counterparty-specific information not intended for public disclosure, it has been withdrawn from public access pending legal, authorship, and publication review.
This is a procedural withdrawal, not a determination on the merits of the underlying document. Parties with an authorized governance or contractual interest in this instrument should contact the Foundation directly through the appropriate internal channel.
